Companion Animal Health & Care Research

Companion-animal care is its own high-value direction: veterinary evidence, safety, palatability, and responsible product development must precede consumer claims.

The evidence is early, but relevant

Compared with crop, fish, and livestock research, companion-animal evidence is limited. One published dog wound-model study investigated oral hydrogen-rich water and reported differences in healing-related histology, oxidative-stress markers, and gene expression. This is a research result in a defined model, not evidence that a consumer pet-water product treats disease.

Medical-care and wellness development paths

Potential workstreams include veterinary wound-care materials, skin and oral-care formats, recovery-support research, functional drinking-water development, and nutrition compatibility. Every route requires veterinary review, species-specific safety work, palatability testing, stability testing, and an evidence-led claims strategy.

A practical market-development lens

Latin America is a major companion-animal market with expanding functional nutrition and wellness categories. MUQI can explore the material side of future water, food, and care-product concepts: hydrogen release, silicon/mineral compatibility, packaging stability, and pilot protocols. We do not translate human claims directly into pet products.
