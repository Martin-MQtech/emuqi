Second Hydrogen Agriculture High-Quality Development Forum

Kunshan, Jiangsu · presentation materials and on-site documentation shared by MUQI / Martin Chen.

Why this forum matters

Hydrogen agriculture is moving from isolated product concepts toward an interdisciplinary discussion spanning crop science, soil health, material engineering, water systems, animal biology, and agricultural commercialization. Industry forums provide a place to compare technical routes, evidence standards, pilot conditions, and real implementation questions.

MUQI participated in the Second Hydrogen Agriculture High-Quality Development Forum in Kunshan and published presentation and on-site materials through its public WeChat account. The source record is retained here as an industry-event reference within the Hydrogen Agriculture Hub.

MUQI perspective

MUQI's contribution is a material-platform perspective: solid-state hydrogen, silicon-based mineral materials, functional fertilizer concepts, water-treatment integration, and measurable cultivation trials. The goal is to collaborate with agronomy, fertilizer, irrigation, breeding, and farming partners rather than reduce hydrogen agriculture to a single product claim.

What this entry is not

This page records an industry exchange. It does not treat forum participation, slides, or on-site materials as independent scientific validation. Research claims remain linked to the original studies and institutions listed in the Hub resource directory.
