Hydrogen & Aquaculture Research

A research context for molecular hydrogen in fish culture and material platforms inside real water-circulation systems.

A recent fish-culture study

A 2024 study in juvenile largemouth bass compared hydrogen-rich-water concentrations and reported differences in growth, survival, feed-conversion, digestive activity, antioxidant indicators, mTOR-related genes, and gut microbiota. This finding supports further controlled research; it is not a universal outcome for all species or operating conditions.

What a credible pilot controls

Aquaculture trials need dissolved-hydrogen measurement alongside oxygen, temperature, pH, alkalinity, ammonia, nitrite, stocking density, feed, water exchange, and species-specific welfare metrics. A module that integrates with an existing recirculating system is preferable to a technology that changes daily operator routines.

MUQI application route

MUQI can explore solid-state hydrogen media, silicon and mineral carriers, cartridge or bypass modules, and water-treatment integration for hatcheries, recirculating systems, ornamental fish, shrimp, and finfish pilots.
