What Is the "Ten Questions, One Industry" Series?

The hydrogen health market is full of adjectives: miracle, energizing, antioxidant, life-changing. What it lacks is a shared set of calm questions. That is why we produced Ten Questions, One Industry (氢健康十问) — a ten-episode video series, released in both Chinese and English, that treats hydrogen health as a manufacturing and trade business rather than a mystique.

Each episode asks one question and answers it with definitions, published evidence, supply-chain logic and standards tracking. The goal is not to sell a dream, but to give consumers, distributors, R&D engineers and industry watchers a shared, checkable vocabulary for this fast-growing category.

What Makes This Series Different?

Four Editorial Disciplines

  • Definitions before hype: every episode starts by fixing what words like "hydrogen water", "concentration" and "evidence" actually mean.
  • Evidence discipline: published research and seller claims are always separated; no therapeutic promises are made anywhere in the series.
  • Unit economics: the series treats the category as manufacturing + trade — dissolution, materials, cost, channels and pricing.
  • Standards ceiling: the final episodes look at certification and standards, because the ceiling of this business is written in standards documents.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers for viewers, distributors and industry analysts.

Q: What is the "Ten Questions, One Industry" video series?

A: It is a 10-episode bilingual video series by MUQI Tech (Martin Chen) that examines the hydrogen health industry through ten calm questions — from business definitions and evidence quality to customs codes and standards. It is published on YouTube in Chinese (氢健康十问) and English (TEN QUESTIONS, ONE INDUSTRY), 20 episodes in total.

Q: Is hydrogen water proven to work?

A: The series separates published research from marketing claims. Evidence quality varies by outcome and study design, so we recommend checking where a study was published, how large the sample was, and whether different claims use comparable definitions before drawing conclusions.

Q: Why do hydrogen water products differ so much in concentration?

A: Generation technology, core materials, water volume, temperature and measurement methods all affect dissolved H2 levels. Episode 4 walks through a real market spread as wide as 30,000x between products that all call themselves hydrogen-rich.

Q: Can concentration claims from different products be compared directly?

A: Not directly. Always check the testing method and conditions (temperature, time, measurement standard) first; concentration claims made under different protocols are not directly comparable.
