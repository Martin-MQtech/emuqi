# Emuqi SEO/GEO audit brief

Date: 2026-07-31
Audience: Emuqi website owner and content/marketing team

## Question

Why are many Emuqi pages not appearing in Google, what current SEO/GEO problems can be identified from the repository and live site, and what phased actions should be taken before paid traffic and social distribution?

## Scope

In scope: crawlability, indexability signals, sitemap/robots/canonical/hreflang, titles and meta descriptions, headings and wording, structured data, internal linking, content quality and evidence boundaries, GEO/citation readiness, social distribution, Google Search Console and paid-traffic sequencing.

Out of scope for this first pass: changing code, submitting Search Console requests, launching ads, posting to social platforms, or claiming actual Google index status without Search Console access.

## Assumptions

- The site is primarily static HTML served at emuqi.com.
- The repository is the source of truth for page inventory; live fetches are used to detect deployment/cache mismatches.
- “Not indexed” may mean technical exclusion, crawl delay, duplicate/canonical selection, or Google quality/indexing decisions; only Search Console URL Inspection can distinguish these reliably.

## Angles

1. Local technical inventory and metadata audit.
2. Live crawlability, canonical deployment, sitemap, robots, and representative URL checks.
3. Google official indexing and Search guidance.
4. Content quality, wording, evidence, and GEO citation readiness.
5. Social distribution and paid-search sequencing.
