Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many hydrogen ceramic balls should I use per bottle?

For MUQI H2-BALL-PRO solid-state hydrogen ceramic spheres, the standard sizing ratio is 30–50 g per 500 ml of water (rapid 5-minute activation). Use the ICR calculator on this page to estimate the exact dissolved hydrogen (ppb), ORP, pH and cartridge lifespan for your own bottle geometry — the kinetic model penalizes under-dosing below a 0.05 g/ml ratio.

How much MACA-KDF antimicrobial media do I need for a humidifier or robot vacuum tank?

Base dosing is 8 g/L for static water (pet fountains, ice machines), 10 g/L for ultrasonic humidifiers, 12 g/L for robot-vacuum dirty-water tanks, and 25 g/L for under-sink RO carbon blocks, scaled by tank liters and target months, with a 15 g minimum charge. Example: a 1.5 L humidifier tank at 12 months needs 10 × 1.5 × (12/12) = 15 g of MACA-KDF-5MM media.

Are the calculator results medical claims?

No. All outputs are engineering design targets computed by the MUQI ICR Crystalline Hydrolysis Kinetic Equation v2.4 for consumer wellness materials and appliance water circuits. They are not disease diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention claims. Re-validate under your actual water chemistry before making any market claim.

Can AI agents call these calculators directly?

Yes. The same equations are exposed as WebMCP tools — calculate_hydrogen_performance and calculate_antimicrobial_dosage — in the machine-readable tool manifest at https://www.emuqi.com/assets/js/muqi-webmcp.js, alongside search_muqi_materials, get_compliance_certificates and submit_b2b_rfq, so AI assistants can compute dosages without hallucinating numbers.
