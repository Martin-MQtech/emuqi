03 / Resources directory

Tools and sources for the next check.

This is a working directory, not a ranked list. Use the category that matches the question, open the maintained source, and verify the exact record, scope, or study before making a decision.

Find papers, institutions, and specialist discovery routes. Search results are leads; the original study is the source.

02

Plant, livestock, aquaculture, and companion-animal research

Use these routes to track hydrogen biology across crops, rumen systems, fish culture, poultry, veterinary care, and functional nutrition. Species-specific evidence matters.

Global · plant studyFrontiers: strawberry salt-stress study

Hydrogen-rich water, biomass, ion balance, oxidative stress, and root endophytes.

↗
China · field trialHydrogen nanobubble water and cherry tomatoes

Two-year field research on yield, quality, soil nutrients, and fertilizer interaction.

↗
Australia · livestock researchMLA: hydrogen balance in livestock

Rumen microbiology, feed utilization, hydrogen pathways, and methane research priorities.

↗
Global · livestock reviewHydrogen and livestock biology review

Production performance, stress tolerance, gut microbiota, and future research directions.

↗
Global · aquaculture studyHydrogen-rich water and largemouth bass

Growth, survival, digestion, antioxidant indicators, signaling pathways, and gut microbiota.

↗
Global · veterinary studyHydrogen-rich water in a dog wound model

Early companion-animal evidence; useful for research mapping, not a consumer product claim.
