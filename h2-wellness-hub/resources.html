03 / DIRECTORY OF PRIMARY SOURCES

Start your next diligence check from primary authority platforms.

A practical working directory for product engineers, regulatory teams, investors, and procurement operators. Skip secondary repackaging—go directly to the maintained databases responsible for primary records.

01 Research & Literature 02 Clinical Trials 03 Standards & Regulation 04 Testing & Labs 05 Patents & IP
💡 Principles & Boundary Statement: All directories listed below connect to independent, third-party public databases and institutional portals. Search results represent research clues or regulatory filings, not commercial efficacy endorsements. A patent filing does not prove commercial performance, and standard compliance must be verified against exact published scope numbers.
01

Biomedical Literature & Academic Knowledge Sources

Discover peer-reviewed papers, academic institutes, and mechanistic biology archives. Search outputs are leads; the original publication is the ground truth.

Global · Biomedical Database

PubMed (NLM / NIH) Molecular Hydrogen Search

The global benchmark for peer-reviewed biomedical literature covering molecular hydrogen mechanisms, animal models, and human trials.

💡 Search Terms: "molecular hydrogen", "hydrogen-rich water", "hydrogen inhalation"
Search PubMed ↗
Japan · Scientific Database

J-STAGE (Japan Science and Technology Agency)

Maintained by the Japanese government to index academic journals, terminology, and research across hydrogen medicine, water biology, and material science.

💡 Search Terms: 水素, 水素水, molecular hydrogen medicine
Access J-STAGE ↗
China · University Center

SJTU Hydrogen Science Center (Prof. Xuejun Sun)

Key Chinese academic portal for gas biology and hydrogen medicine research, documenting cellular mechanisms and clinical translation progress.

💡 Focus Areas: Selective antioxidant mechanisms, mitochondrial signaling, dissolved H2 kinetics
View Institutional Profile ↗
Japan · Medical School Hub

Keio University Center for Molecular Hydrogen Medicine

Translational research initiative at Keio Global Research Institute (KGRI) exploring acute hydrogen inhalation in cardiopulmonary and neuro-care.

💡 Focus Areas: Acute inhalation clinical designs, emergency medicine protocols
Visit Keio Project Portal ↗
China · Long-term Archive

ScienceNet "Molecular Hydrogen Medicine" Archive

Longest-running Chinese-language academic commentary archive dissecting published papers, clinical methodologies, and gas biology trends.

💡 Intended Audience: R&D scientists, formulators, and technical product managers
Browse ScienceNet Archive ↗
Global · Discovery Tool

H2 Research Database

Categorized index of molecular hydrogen publications structured by physiological organ systems and administration routes (inhalation, water, saline).

💡 Feature: Fast filtering by administration route and disease model
Open H2 Research DB ↗
02

Human Clinical Trials & Protocol Registries

Verify registration IDs, study designs, participant criteria, sponsors, endpoints, and posted results. Registration is not approval.

Global · NIH Trial Registry

ClinicalTrials.gov (US National Library of Medicine)

The world's largest registry of clinical studies, tracking randomized controlled trials (RCTs), dosing regimens, and verified study completions.

💡 Search: Query "molecular hydrogen" or "hydrogen water" for exact NCT identifiers
Search ClinicalTrials.gov ↗
China · Primary Registry

Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR)

Primary WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP) partner in China, indexing registered clinical protocols and university-led studies.

💡 Search: Look up registration numbers using keywords "分子氢", "富氢水", "hydrogen"
Enter ChiCTR Registry ↗
Japan · MHLW Registry

Japan Registry of Clinical Trials (jRCT / MHLW)

Official Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare registry for clinical research, advanced medical care type B trials, and ethics filings.

💡 Search: Query Japanese terms 水素ガス, 水素吸入療法
Access jRCT Portal ↗
China · Drug Trials

NMPA Drug Clinical Trial Registry

China National Medical Products Administration Center for Drug Evaluation official repository for medical gas and inhalation investigational drug trials.

💡 Use Case: Formal medical device and pharmaceutical compliance diligence
Search Drug Trials DB ↗
03

Standards, Regulations & Market Oversight

Verify national standards, group standards, food production licences, and enforcement notices on official platforms.

China · Standards Platform

National Standards Public Service Platform (SAMR)

Authoritative verification of Chinese National Standards (GB), Industry Standards, and Group Standards—checking status, drafting committees, and scope.

💡 Scopes: Drinking water quality, hydrogen generators, water purifiers, antimicrobial media
Search Standards DB ↗
China · Full Text Standard Access

National Standards Open Access System (OpenSTD)

Official public welfare database providing free full-text inspection of mandatory (GB) and recommended (GB/T) national standards.

💡 Common References: GB 5749 Drinking Water Quality, Food-contact Safety Standards
Read Standard Full Text ↗
China · Medical Device Authority

NMPA Data Search System

Verify medical device registration certificates, technical specifications, and licensed indications to prevent illicit non-medical marketing claims.

💡 Verification: Check whether hydrogen inhalers hold Class II/III medical device certificates
Search NMPA Records ↗
China · Market Regulation

SAMR Public Licence & Inspection Query

Query food production licences (SC codes), special food registrations, and public national sampling/testing compliance bulletins.

💡 Verification: Check pre-packaged hydrogen beverage compliance and production standards
Query SAMR Licences ↗
Global · Industry Self-Regulation

International Hydrogen Standards Association (IHSA)

Self-regulatory industry guidelines covering testing methodologies and concentration thresholds for molecular hydrogen products.

💡 Note: Voluntary industry guidelines; distinct from ISO or governmental regulatory mandates
Visit IHSA Portal ↗
04

Accredited Testing Labs & Certificates

Verify China Metrology Accreditation (CMA) and China National Accreditation Service (CNAS) testing scopes. Accreditation applies strictly to listed parameters.

China · National Lab Accreditation

CNAS Accredited Organization Search System

Official portal to verify testing laboratory accreditation certificates and inspect authorized test parameters and methods.

💡 Verification: Verify whether a laboratory is accredited for gas purity, dissolution ppb, or heavy metal leaching
Search CNAS Labs ↗
Japan · Laboratory Directory

NITE / IAJapan JNLA Laboratory Directory

Directory of Japan Laboratory Accreditation System testing bodies assessing water quality and material physical-chemical parameters.

💡 Use Case: Cross-checking Japanese supply chain testing and material certification bodies
Search JNLA Directory ↗
Global · Third-Party Testing

SGS Document Verification & Authentication

Verify the authenticity of drinking water contact tests (NSF/ANSI 42), toxicology safety reports, and RoHS/REACH compliance files.

💡 How to Check: Enter report number and date to inspect the verified electronic original
Verify SGS Documents ↗
05

Patent Search & Intellectual Property Examination

Examine disclosed patents, claims, and examination statuses across hydrogen generation materials, solid-state media, and electrochemical structures.

China · National Patent Office

CNIPA Patent Search and Analysis System (PSS)

Official CNIPA system for searching Chinese and global invention patents, utility models, IPC classifications, and patent families.

💡 Query: Applicant=(MUQI) AND Title=(hydrogen) or Abstract=(solid-state hydrogen)
Search CNIPA Patents ↗
China · Examination Status

CNIPA Patent Examination Information Query (CPQuery)

Real-time tracking of Chinese patent substantive examination stages, Office Actions (OA), legal fee status, and maintenance validity.

💡 Purpose: Distinguish between "granted and active" patents vs "published / rejected / abandoned" filings
Check Examination Status ↗
Global · Patent Index

Google Patents Multi-Language Search

Global indexing across USPTO, EPO, JPO, and WIPO PCT databases with citation mapping and automated translation.

💡 Advantage: Multi-lingual claims mapping and forward/backward citation network inspection
Search Google Patents ↗
