03 / Resources directory

Tools and sources for the next check.

This is a working directory, not a ranked list. Use the category that matches the question, open the maintained source, and verify the exact record, scope, or study before making a decision.

Find papers, institutions, and specialist discovery routes. Search results are leads; the original study is the source.
