Hydrogen Water Cup

A Hydrogen Water Cup is a portable electrolysis cup that generates molecular hydrogen in water to reach a target concentration. It is one of the most consumer-accessible hydrogen water devices, with significant variation in price, technology, and hydrogen output across market products.

Boundary. Hydrogen concentration, stability, and actual intake vary widely across products. This page does not interpret product existence or labeled specs as a health efficacy guarantee. Consumers should look for independent testing data and labeling standards.
