Where science becomes product, channel, and market.

What this Hub does

We use Hydrogen Wellness Industry for the product, channel, quality, and market category. Healthcare is reserved for hospital, clinical-research, and regulatory contexts. We follow commercial signals, point to credible resources, and collect cases that reveal how the category is actually being built.

Commercial signals worth tracking.

A U.S. industry council enters the hydrogen-wellness category.

The American Hydrogen Health Association launched as a voluntary industry council focused on transparency, scientific guidance, testing, labeling, and responsible marketing. The business signal is category self-organization, not government certification.

United States02 Apr 2026

Source: company distribution announcement · Retail expansion

Completion does not mean a result or consumer claim has been established.
