PHYSICAL BENCHMARKS · INDUSTRY COMPLIANCE THRESHOLDS

Core Quality & Regulatory Benchmarks at a Glance

Separating marketing claims from physical chemistry principles and verified testing standards.

≥ 0.5 ppm
IHSA Therapeutic Threshold
Minimum dissolved concentration recommended by the International Hydrogen Standards Association for human biological efficacy.
1.6 ppm
Ambient Saturation Limit
Theoretical physical saturation of molecular hydrogen in pure water at standard atmospheric pressure and 20°C (~1600 ppb). Higher levels require pressurization or nanobubbles.
≥ 99.99 %
Inhalation Purity Threshold
Mandatory threshold for breathing devices verified by accredited gas chromatography to ensure zero ozone, chlorine, or leached volatiles.
GC-TCD Standard
Analytical Gold Standard
Gas chromatography with thermal conductivity detection (GC-TCD) is the universally accepted gold standard. ORP meters remain qualitative only.
