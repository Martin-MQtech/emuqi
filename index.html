Technical Q&A & Specifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct engineering insights into MUQI solid-state ICR hydrogen generation, antibacterial standards, and custom manufacturing capabilities.

Q: How does MUQI solid-state ICR technology generate dissolved hydrogen?

MUQI's Intelligent Controlled Release (ICR) platform embeds micro-nano magnesium-mineral crystals within a microporous ceramic matrix. When exposed to water, it produces a steady release of nanobubble dissolved hydrogen (600–1200+ ppb) without requiring electric electrolysis or power cables, while dropping water ORP to -200mV ~ -400mV.

Q: What is the antibacterial rate and verification standard for MACA ceramic balls?

MACA ceramic balls deliver an antibacterial rate exceeding 99% against Staphylococcus aureus, Escherichia coli, and Candida albicans. Verified and certified by SGS and the Guangdong Detection Center of Microbiology under SAC/TC621 national standards.

Q: Does MUQI provide custom sintering formulas and OEM/ODM specifications?

Yes. As a direct manufacturer with 37 national invention patents and dedicated sintering kilns in Zibo, MUQI supports custom ball diameters (2mm to 20mm), customized dissolution rates, composite material blending, and private-label commercial packaging.

Q: What safety and food-grade hygiene certifications do MUQI materials hold?

MUQI functional ceramics strictly comply with SGS food-contact safety standards, RoHS environmental requirements, and national drinking water hygiene licenses, ensuring zero heavy metal dissolution and non-toxic water treatment for global appliances.
