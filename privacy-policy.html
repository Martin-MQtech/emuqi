1. Overview & Commitment

Shandong MUQI Health Technology Co., Ltd. ("MUQI Tech", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, security, and confidential business data of our global clients, OEM/ODM partners, and website visitors. We comply with international data privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information through two primary methods:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide (B2B Inquiries & RFQs)

  • Contact Information: Full name, professional corporate email address, contact telephone number, and company name.
  • Project & Engineering Requirements: Technical specifications, desired dissolved hydrogen levels, annual volume estimates, application fields (e.g. water purification, hydrogen beauty, functional wellness), and physical sample delivery addresses.

B. Automatically Collected Technical & Analytical Data

  • Device & Network Identifiers: IP address, browser type and version, language settings, operating system, and referral URLs.

The information we collect is strictly used for legitimate B2B commercial purposes:

5. Data Subject Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold specific legal rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we maintain about you or your business.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to correct inaccurate or incomplete contact records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of your information when it is no longer required for active contract execution or statutory retention.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: You can object to data processing for marketing or analytics purposes at any time.
  • CCPA "Do Not Sell My Information": We do NOT sell, rent, or lease personal or corporate identifiable information to any third parties for monetary consideration.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an official request to our Data Compliance desk at muqizb@gmail.com.

6. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted across www.emuqi.com is secured with TLS/HTTPS 256-bit encryption. We implement strict internal access controls and physical security measures at our Zibo Advanced Ceramics Base to protect confidential formula requests and customer data. We retain inquiry records only as long as necessary to facilitate ongoing commercial partnerships or meet statutory tax and export regulations.
