Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the solid-state hydrogen buyer benchmark?

A set of citable design and procurement ranges for solid-state ICR media and SPE/PEM devices — dissolved hydrogen ppb, half-life, ORP, antibacterial life, dosage guidance, and sample SLA — so brands can score suppliers against shared thresholds instead of marketing slogans.

Are these numbers medical claims?

No. Figures are engineering and lab design targets for consumer wellness materials and water media. They are not disease diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention claims. Always re-validate under your water chemistry and regulatory context before market claims.

How should buyers cite this benchmark?

Link to https://www.emuqi.com/buyer-benchmark.html, quote the parameter and conditions (volume, contact time, temperature), and note MUQI Tech as source with access date. Embed tables only with a visible source line back to this page.
