1. Stepping into the Metrology Frontier: Empirical Rigor Over Market Hype

In advanced materials, "first-tier enterprises build standards, second-tier build brands, and third-tier produce goods." In the rapidly expanding molecular hydrogen wellness sector, certified public metrology infrastructure is the definitive benchmark separating legitimate science from unverified commercial exaggeration.

Delegates conducted an in-depth inspection of specialized calibration cleanrooms at the Shandong Hydrogen Energy New Materials Metrology and Testing Center, observing high-precision primary weight sets, trace-gas chromatography stations, and ultra-high-pressure electrochemical test benches.

Figure 1: Center Exhibition Hall showcasing the full-traceability public metrology service model.

For MUQI Tech, scientific metrology governs every material specification:

  • Ambient Open Saturation Limits: Strictly adhering to Henry's gas solubility laws, MUQI benchmarks effervescent dissolved hydrogen at 1,200 to 1,500 ppb in ambient open water, backed by certified third-party testing;
  • Sustained Negative ORP: Datalogging sustained oxidation-reduction potential between -300mV to -600mV across dissolution curves;
  • Biocompatibility & Safety: Full ICP-MS elemental mass spectrometry verifying zero toxic heavy-metal leeching for skin contact and oral intake.

2. Government-Industry-Academia Synergy

The plenary symposium brought together regulatory authorities and industry peers. The meeting was moderated by Chen Chao (Zibo Market Regulation Bureau), with welcoming remarks from Jing Gang (President of Zibo Metrology Research Institute). Key strategic addresses were delivered by Zhou Kun (Deputy Director of Market Regulation) and Zhang Haisheng (Vice Chairman of Federation of Industry and Commerce), followed by a policy lecture by Zhu Feng (President of Standardization Research Institute).

3. The 'MUQI Inside' Vision: Scaling Solid-State Hydrogen

By replacing bulky, cord-bound electrolysis equipment with electricity-free solid-state hydrogen new materials, MUQI Tech empowers global brands across three primary verticals:

  1. Hydrogen-Infused Continuous Nonwovens (150cm roll-stock): Moisture-activated release (>1300 ppb, >30 mins) for medical cold-compress dressings and sanitary napkins with zero machinery modifications on high-speed factory lines;
  2. Solid-State Mineral Effervescent Media: Portable travel infusers, tea tumblers, and fruit wash tablets with zero ozone/chlorine byproducts;
  3. Consumable Cartridges for Smart Wellness Hardware: Capsule-based facial steamers and bubbling foot spa tablets that unlock recurring consumable revenue for appliance brands.
