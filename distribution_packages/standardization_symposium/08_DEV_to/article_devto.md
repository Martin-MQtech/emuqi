On the 57th World Standards Day, the Zibo Administration for Market Regulation and the Municipal Federation of Industry and Commerce organized the **Hydrogen Industry Standardization Symposium** at the Shandong Hydrogen Energy New Materials Metrology and Testing Center.

**MUQI Technology (MUQI Tech)** participated as Vice President unit of the Zibo Hydrogen Chamber of Commerce, exploring the engineering intersection between public metrology calibration, proton exchange membrane (PEM) national standard revisions, and the industrial scaling of solid-state hydrogen new materials.

---

### The Engineering Imperative: Public Metrology Over Marketing Hype

In emerging functional materials, public metrology infrastructure is the definitive benchmark separating verified physics from commercial exaggeration.

At the testing center, engineers demonstrated primary mass standards, trace-gas chromatography, and cyclic ultra-high-pressure electrochemical workstations supporting **over 200 public metrology standards**.

![Center Tasks Exhibition](https://www.emuqi.com/assets/images/blog/standards-symposium/center-exhibition-tasks.webp)

For solid-state hydrogen materials, our engineering protocols enforce three critical baselines:
1. **Henry's Law Saturation Limits:** Under ambient temperature and atmospheric pressure, open dissolved hydrogen naturally reaches physical near-saturation at **1,200 to 1,500 ppb**. We reject unscientific claims exceeding tens of thousands of ppb in open cups, backing all claims with certified third-party testing.
2. **Sustained Electrochemical ORP:** Datalogging oxidation-reduction potential to guarantee stable negative reduction capacity between -300mV and -600mV across dissolution curves.
3. **Trace Elemental Safety:** Inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS) to ensure zero heavy metal leaching in oral and dermal applications.

### PEM Electrolysis & National Standard Synergy

The testing center actively provides empirical validation for the revision of national standards governing **Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) water electrolysis systems**.

![PEM National Standard Revision Board](https://www.emuqi.com/assets/images/blog/standards-symposium/pem-standard-revision-board.webp)

This aligns with MUQI Tech's broader standards engagement. As a standing member of the **National Technical Committee on Antimicrobial Surface Performance Standardization (SAC/TC621)**—which mirrors the International Organization for Standardization's **ISO/TC330**—we actively bridge materials synthesis with formal standards drafting.

- **Modular Consumable Cartridges:** Capsule upgrades for high-power facial steamers and foot-bath appliances, transforming commodity electronics into recurring consumable ecosystems.
