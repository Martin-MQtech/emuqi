Leading with Standards: MUQI Tech Participates in Hydrogen Industry Standardization Symposium to Anchor Public Metrology & Quality Infrastructure

October 15, 2026 · By MUQI Tech Research Team · Category: Industry Standards & Materials Science

Executive Summary: On October 14, 2026, marking the 57th World Standards Day, the Zibo Administration for Market Regulation and the Federation of Industry and Commerce convened the "Hydrogen Industry Standardization Symposium" at Shandong Hydrogen Energy New Materials Metrology and Testing Center. MUQI Tech attended as Vice President unit, advancing 200+ public metrology standards and national PEM electrolysis standards revision.

1. Stepping into the Metrology Frontier: Grounding Hydrogen in Empirical Rigor

In modern manufacturing, "top-tier enterprises establish standards, second-tier build brands, and third-tier produce goods." In the molecular hydrogen wellness sector, public metrology infrastructure serves as the ultimate litmus test separating scientific reality from unfounded marketing claims.

Delegates surveyed specialized cleanrooms supporting over 200 public metrology standards, from gas chromatography to nanovolt electrochemical workstations. For MUQI Tech, scientific metrology governs every material specification:

  • Differentiating Ambient Open Saturation from Pressurized Peaks: Calibrating ambient effervescent dissolved hydrogen at the true physical ceiling of 1,200 to 1,500 ppb;
  • Continuous Oxidation-Reduction Potential (ORP): Maintaining stable negative potential (-300mV to -600mV) over extended dissolution curves;
  • Rigorous Biocompatibility & ICP-MS Testing: Full elemental mass spectrometry ensuring zero heavy-metal risk.

2. Government-Industry-Academia Consensus

Roundtable dialogue sessions focused on standard-setting policy, testing collaboration, and supply chain resilience, hosted by Chen Chao (Market Regulation) with addresses from Jing Gang, Zhou Kun, Zhang Haisheng, and Zhu Feng.

3. Downstreaming the Technology: The 'MUQI Inside' Model

By replacing bulky, cord-bound electrolysis machines with electricity-free solid-state hydrogen new materials, MUQI Tech powers three major commercial verticals: Hydrogen-Infused Continuous Nonwovens (150cm roll-stock), Solid-State Mineral Effervescent Media (1,200~1,500 ppb), and Consumable Cartridges for Smart Wellness Hardware.
