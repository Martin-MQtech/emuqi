### 1. Facebook 动态主文案 (Main Story Copy)

```text
A conversation with a hardware factory owner last month really stuck with me.

He runs an injection molding and assembly plant in Guangdong, exporting over 800,000 foot spa buckets every year to North America, Europe, and domestic e-commerce brands.

He showed me their latest flagship:
Microcomputer PID temperature control, dual motorized 3D kneading rollers, wave jets, UV-C sterilizer, WiFi connectivity. A truly impressive piece of electrical engineering.

Then he sighed:
"Martin, we're trapped. Five years ago, we sold these for $42 wholesale. Today, with all these smart chips and massage motors added, clients still beat us down to $28. Every time a container ships, that's the end of the transaction. If the machine lasts 4 years, we don't see another dime from that customer for 4 years."

To help brand clients convert buyers, they throw in two cheap herbal foot soak bags in every box. 

Then came the shocking 3·15 lab investigation:
Independent testing across 9 best-selling e-commerce herbal foot soak bags found:
- 100% contained severe fungal contamination;
- Fungal colony counts exceeded cosmetic safety limits by up to 160 times;
- Bags were stuffed with crushed plant debris, dirt clods, and unsterilized filler.

Think about the irony:
A consumer spends $60 on a high-tech, precision-engineered wellness foot bath, drops in a complimentary $0.05 herbal bag, and ends up soaking in 160x fungal water that clogs the drainage valve and stains the plastic tub.

That complimentary gift is no longer a perk—it’s an active liability to the hardware brand.

When you look at Western markets on Amazon, the playbook is completely different:
Appliance giants like HoMedics and Renpho mastered the "Razor & Blades" model years ago. The hardware is just the entry point ($40-$80). The real profit engine is the monthly replenishment of therapeutic mineral salts and effervescent tablets (Epsom salt hydrotherapy alone is a $2.8B global market).

And today, solid-state molecular hydrogen is taking that flywheel to the next level:
1. Zero Electrical Modification: No need to redesign boilers or void CE/UL safety certifications with expensive electrolysis chambers;
2. Pristine Micro-Nano Bubbles: Dissolves in seconds into crystal-clear water with zero sludge, zero mess, and zero odor;
3. Verifiable Hard Science: 1200+ ppb measurable dissolved H2, backed by peer-reviewed transdermal absorption studies (like Iwai et al.'s 2023 porcine immersion research).

The unit economics are simple:
If an active foot bath user soaks 8–12 times a month, they consume 1 box of tablets monthly. Reordering 6–10 boxes a year at $9.99 adds $60–$100 in high-margin recurring consumable GMV per machine.

For hardware manufacturers and wellness brand founders, the era of competing solely on razor-thin hardware margins is over. The future belongs to brands that build a recurring wellness ritual.

Full engineering whitepaper: https://www.emuqi.com/blog/solid-state-hydrogen-foot-spa-consumables-en.html
```
