From the CEO's seat, the hardest part isn't proving hydrogen works on crops — 1,500+ papers have settled that. The hard part is what comes after the paper: a common measurement ruler, a formulation a farmer can actually apply, a cost ledger per mu, and someone to train the field.

That's why our session with Prof. Pei Zhou's team at SJTU mattered. We didn't debate "whether" — we aligned on "how": route alignment, evidence boundaries, and a joint field-validation plan.

The next 3 years belong to the teams that solve delivery.
