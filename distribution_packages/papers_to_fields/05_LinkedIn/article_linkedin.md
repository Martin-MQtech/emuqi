# From Papers to Fields: What 1,500 Papers Mean for Hydrogen Agriculture — MUQI × SJTU Hydrogen Science Center

*November 3, 2026 · By Martin Chen, Partner & CEO, Shandong MUQI Health Technology Co., Ltd. (MUQI Tech)*

**Hydrogen agriculture is not a new track — it is a spillover.** More than 1,500 papers worldwide, roughly 90% from China, still add up to zero independent statistical calibers. The bottleneck is delivery, not evidence.

**On 29 October 2026** our team spent an afternoon with Professor Pei Zhou's team at the SJTU Hydrogen Science Center (Agri-Innovation Building B501). We agreed on three things: route alignment, evidence boundaries, and a joint field-validation plan.

[Figure 1 — SJTU meeting photo]: https://www.emuqi.com/assets/images/blog/papers-to-fields/photo-discussion.jpg

**Three observations worth your time:**

1. **The evidence is abundant — the delivery is missing.** Paper → product → commodity → industry: each stage drops controls, replication, cost ledgers, standards, testing, insurance. What the field lacks is a common ruler: what to measure, how, at what dosage.

2. **Scenario decides the route.** Devices fit powered, piped, high-value protected agriculture (Japan's Suilive SA-1; HERO's US$16,311 unit — which promises no yield gains and requires blank controls). Energy hydrogen decarbonises fertiliser and machinery — that is input decarbonisation, not agronomy (NSW's A$45.2M hub). China's 90% of papers run on a material route: solid materials straight to unpowered, dispersed, low-cost fields.

[Figure 2 — three routes]: https://www.emuqi.com/assets/images/blog/papers-to-fields/chart-routes-en.jpg

3. **Profit sits at both ends of the smile curve** — materials/formulation and scenario/brand — not in device assembly. That is why MUQI does not build machines: we turn hydrogen from a hazardous gas into a transportable, blendable, repurchasable solid material (37 patents, 15 invention patents; lab co-built with the Technical Institute of Physics and Chemistry, CAS).

[Figure 3 — smile curve]: https://www.emuqi.com/assets/images/blog/papers-to-fields/chart-smile-curve-en.jpg

The SJTU team's six questions all targeted delivery: gas composition, release products, silicon fractions, control granules, encapsulation release, batch particle size. Not interrogation — the standard due-diligence before anything enters a field. Our written replies are attached to the joint record.
