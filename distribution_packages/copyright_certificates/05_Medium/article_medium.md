### 1. Why National-Level Literary Work Copyright Carries Decisive Legal Weight

Unlike provincial filings, registrations designated with the prefix **"国作登字" (National Registration)** undergo strict substantive examination by the Copyright Protection Center of China (CPCC) in Beijing.

In technical and engineering disciplines, a Literary Work Copyright protects:
* **Theoretical Foundations**: The mathematical models, chemical balance equations, and kinetic reaction curves governing solid-state hydrogen generation ($MgH_2 + 2H_2O \rightarrow Mg(OH)_2 + H_2 \uparrow$).
* **Process Documentation**: The micro-architectural specifications of microporous ceramic matrices, including sintering thermal curves, pore-size gradient distributions, and sustained-release flow rate equations.
* **Instructional Manuals & Whitepapers**: Comprehensive engineering datasheets and quality inspection standards supplied to downstream OEM brands.

This registration legally shields MUQI Tech's proprietary literature against unauthorized reproduction, technical plagiarism, and deceptive marketing by uncertified third parties across both domestic and international markets.

### 2. The Engineering Substance: What is ICR™ Technology?

**ICR™ (Intelligent Control Dissolution Release)** is MUQI Tech's proprietary materials engineering platform designed to overcome the core technical limitation of traditional hydrogen media: *uncontrolled, short-lived, violent gas surging followed by rapid depletion.*

Traditional magnesium-based granules react violently upon contact with water, discharging the majority of dissolved gas within minutes, followed by surface passivating oxidation that ceases reaction completely.

```text
Traditional Media:   [Violent Spike >3000 ppb] ──> [Rapid Depletion to 0 ppb in 2h]
ICR™ Technology:     [Regulated Saturation 1200–1500 ppb] ──> [Sustained 18–24h Half-Life]
```

![Certificate Gallery](https://www.emuqi.com/assets/images/blog/copyright-certificates/certificate-comparison-gallery.webp)

ICR™ technology solves this through three fundamental innovations:
1. **Porous Ceramic Matrix Encapsulation**: Active catalytic reagents are embedded within an inorganic silicate-aluminate micro-porous ceramic framework with calibrated pore diameters ($0.1 \sim 10\,\mu\text{m}$).
2. **Thermodynamic Equilibrium Control**: Regulating water ingress to maintain stable dissolved hydrogen near open-air physical saturation (**1,200 to 1,500 ppb**) at ambient temperature and pressure.
3. **Negative ORP Stabilization**: Maintaining steady electrochemical reduction capacity ($-300\,\text{mV}$ to $-600\,\text{mV}$) over an extended 18–24 hour half-life, resisting rapid outgassing.
