1. Executive Prelude: The Raw Material Paradigm Shift

In one sentence: MUQI Technology has engineered solid-state molecular hydrogen into a flexible, breathable textile.

This is not another hydrogen-generating water bottle, nor is it a dissolution tablet. It is an industrial raw material format that can be precision slit, rolled, die-cut, and laminated directly into automated converting lines. Through a proprietary low-temperature ion-plating process, solid-state hydrogen-generating elements (HI hydrogen donor) are permanently embedded into non-woven microfibers and multilayer functional matrices.

Stored completely dry and chemically stable on warehouse shelves, the material triggers only when exposed to moisture. Within laboratory setups, sustained molecular hydrogen generation exceeds 30 minutes, producing verifiable dissolved H₂ concentrations measurable by portable and electrochemical dissolved hydrogen meters.

Why is this critical for B2B contract manufacturers, medical device factories, and brand executives? Because this single roll of fabric concurrently addresses two saturated consumer sectors:

Dry-state hydrogen membranes offer on-demand freshness and stable shelf-life.
  • Feminine Care Brand Owners: Standard functional chips integrate directly into high-speed rotary sanitary napkin converting lines, providing a zero-retooling functional upgrade that complies with the latest sanitary mandates.

We do not sell consumer sheet masks; we provide brand partners with the proprietary ability to "Infuse Hydrogen" (MUQI Inside) into their core product lines.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the hydrogen membrane maintain shelf stability without premature hydrogen outgassing?

Unlike liquid pre-soaked masks where dissolved hydrogen dissipates through packaging within hours, MUQI's hydrogen-infused membrane cloth stores hydrogen in a dry, chemically stable solid-state donor form (HI element) embedded via ion-plating. It remains inert during storage and activates exclusively upon contact with moisture (water, essence, or physiological fluids), generating fresh, high-concentration molecular hydrogen on demand.

Can feminine hygiene manufacturers integrate hydrogen chips without retooling high-speed production lines?

Yes. Modern sanitary pad converting lines utilize automated rotary insertion modules originally engineered for anion or far-infrared functional chips. MUQI supplies the hydrogen-infused material pre-slit into standard chip dimensions (or 150cm master rolls), allowing seamless integration into the core absorption composite without line downtime or mechanical retooling.

What measurable metrics validate the hydrogen release performance of this fabric?

In standard laboratory testing (1 sheet immersed in 50ml deionized water), dissolved molecular hydrogen exceeds 1300 ppb within 5 minutes. Continuous kinetic testing demonstrates sustained release curves of 1885 ppb at 5 min, 1774 ppb at 10 min, and 1648 ppb at 30 min, accompanied by a significant reduction in Oxidation-Reduction Potential (ORP) into negative millivolt territory.

What are the regulatory boundaries for marketing claims on hydrogen membrane products?

B2B commercial copy must strictly distinguish between verified material characteristics and consumer efficacy. Brands may legally claim physical composition, moisture-triggered hydrogen release, measured ppb curves, softness, and breathability under cosmetic or hygiene standards (such as GB 15979-2024). Therapeutic, anti-inflammatory, or disease-curing claims are prohibited. Foundational molecular biology (such as Ohsawa et al., Nature Medicine 2007) is cited purely as academic literature, and medical dressings require statutory medical device registrations.
