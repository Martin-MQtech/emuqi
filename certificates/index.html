FAQ

Which third-party laboratories test MUQI ceramic media?

Two independent bodies anchor MUQI's testing: SGS issued the Toxicology & Leaching Safety Test Report against US NSF/ANSI 42 extraction safety requirements (zero toxic heavy metal leaching — Pb, Cd, As, Hg, Cr all below 0.001 ppm, 100% food-contact safe), and the Guangdong Detection Center of Microbiology inspected antimicrobial performance under QB/T 2591-2003, certifying 99.99% elimination of Staphylococcus aureus and Escherichia coli with zero-order controlled release.

What is MUQI's role in China's SAC/TC621 antimicrobial standards committee?

MUQI is a first-batch standing committee member and standard drafting organization of the National Standardization Technical Committee on Antimicrobial Surface Performance (SAC/TC621), with CEO Mr. Martin Chen serving in the committee. SAC/TC621 is the direct Chinese mirror committee to ISO/TC 330 (Surfaces with Biocidal and Antimicrobial Properties).

How many patents does MUQI hold?

MUQI holds 37 patents registered with China's National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA), including 15 invention patents. Flagship patents include ZL 2018 1 0495832.X (solid hydrogen generation material) and ZL 2020 2 1589324.8 (microporous sintered alloy ball).
